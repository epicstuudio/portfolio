02 · about — the person behind the redlines

Nine years in, I still start every project the same way: with a question, not a screen. What does this product owe the person using it — and what's the shortest honest path to delivering that?

I've designed for fintech, commerce, and health, from scrappy 0→1 launches to systems used by millions. My happy place is the messy middle: where research, business constraints, and craft collide — and the outline becomes the outcome.

Based in Dubai, working with teams everywhere.
